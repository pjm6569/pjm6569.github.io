Syncly

Syncly is an Android app that automatically syncs folders on your phone with your cloud storage: Google Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, FTP and SFTP.

Google Drive access

Syncly asks for access to your Google Drive so it can upload, update and delete the files in the folders you choose to sync. Everything happens directly between your device and Google. Syncly has no server and never sends your data anywhere else.

Syncly's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
