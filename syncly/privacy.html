Syncly Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-09-30

Summary

Syncly runs entirely on your device. It has no backend server. Your files and account credentials are never sent to the developer or to any third party.

Data Syncly accesses

  • Google Drive files (scope https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive): to upload, update, download and delete files in the folders you select for synchronization. Full Drive access is needed because Syncly must sync files that already exist in your Drive, which the narrower drive.file scope cannot access.
  • Files in local folders you choose on your device.
  • Other services you connect (OneDrive, Dropbox, FTP, SFTP): the same files, sent directly from your device to the service you configured.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is transferred directly between your device and the cloud service. It does not pass through any server operated by the developer.
  • Sign-in tokens and settings are stored locally on your device only.
  • Syncly does not collect analytics, advertising identifiers or usage data.
  • Syncly does not sell, share or transfer your data to third parties.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Syncly's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data obtained through Google APIs is used only to provide the user-facing sync feature, is not transferred to others, is not used for advertising, and is not read by humans.

Deleting your data

Sign out inside the app to remove stored tokens, or uninstall the app to remove all local data. You can also revoke Syncly's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already synced remain in your own cloud storage until you delete them there.
